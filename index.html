About

Designing with purpose
since 2008.

With over 13 years in tech and digital design, I bring a diverse skill set that spans advertising, product, and experience design. I started in high-profile agencies — concepting, pitching, art directing — then made a deliberate shift toward human-centred product work that aligns closer with my values: solving complicated problems, elegantly.

I balance customer needs with business objectives across every project, from voice interfaces and design systems to solar platforms and compliance flows.
